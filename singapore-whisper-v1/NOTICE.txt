Both are based on OpenAI Whisper Small (MIT, Copyright (c) 2022 OpenAI).
LocalFlow modifications: HF safetensors converted to GGML with whisper.cpp v1.9.4;
decoder context 448; original FP32 small tensors preserved; larger tensors FP16;
mobile files quantized to Q5_1. No additional training or accuracy claim.

Model weights are optional downloads, not part of the APK. English transcription
is forced for these experimental models. No Mandarin/code-switch support or
Samsung speed/accuracy is claimed. Compare raw results before relying on them.
